# Commercial position

## Market as

**The private place that catches a memory before it disappears.**

Not a large collection of grief prompts. Not a cheaper therapist. Not a digital version of a 213-page journal.

## Best copy

| Role | Copy |
|------|------|
| Headline | Save the little things before time carries them away. |
| Supporting | Write what you still wish you could say. Preserve the stories, photos, voices, recipes, signs, and ordinary details you never want to lose. |
| Subscription | For $4.99 a month, your memories are privately backed up, easy to find, rich with photos and voices, and ready to become a keepsake whenever you choose. |

## Final recommendation

Keep the emotional foundation and privacy rules from the PRD. Cut the number of visible choices, move account creation until after the first saved memory, place voice and quick capture at the center, and make keepsake creation the premium reason to subscribe.

A bestseller cannot be guaranteed. The strongest route is clear: make the app feel less like a grief program and more like a beautiful, private rescue net for memories that arrive without warning.
